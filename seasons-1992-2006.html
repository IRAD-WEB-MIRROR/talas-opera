A smaller company, 1992–2006

Director: Ivan Medvedev (until 1994), Gulnara Turdubaeva (1994–2009). Chief conductor: Kubanych Toktosunov (until 1995), Sergei Ten (1995–2009).

Half the company left in 1992 and after. The theatre kept the repertoire alive with a smaller ballet and an opera chorus of students, and an audience that is still mostly Russian-speaking.
