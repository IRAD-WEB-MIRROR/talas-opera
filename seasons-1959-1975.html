Aikan’s years, 1959–1975

Director: Akmatbek Usubaliev (1959–1971), Jumagul Sarieva (1971–1983). Chief conductor: Pavel Grinberg (1959–1977).

The house opened in 1959 with the first fly tower in Talas. Aikan Borubai kyzy was its first prima ballerina until 1975; the Ballet School opened in 1962, and in 1974 Asan Jumabaev wrote the ballet Janyl for her.
