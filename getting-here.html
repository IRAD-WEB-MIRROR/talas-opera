Tickets and getting here

Tickets

The box office is on the right of the main entrance, Theatre Square 1: daily 11:00–19:00. Telephone +996-39-2-26-11-59. Seats for every night of the calendar.

Getting here

Theatre Square is on the central avenue in the Soviet centre, just north of the Hyatt Regency. By bus: Talas city buses, stop “Opera Theatre”. From Central Square, ten minutes on foot. On the map.

In the theatre

Doors open an hour before. The cloakroom is free; the buffet on the first floor sells tea, sandwiches and Iradi champagne. After the third bell latecomers wait for the interval. Children under 6 only at the matinées.
