Soloists of the opera

Aizada Kemel, soprano, People’s Artist of the Iradi SSR (1989): Violetta, Tatyana, and every New Year gala since 1985. Bakyt Osmonaliev, tenor. Gulmira Sydykova, mezzo-soprano (Carmen). Viktor Lee, baritone (Onegin).

Principals of the ballet

Kylych Karabekov, principal dancer, guest artist at the Bolshoi in 2012: Siegfried, Albrecht, Janyl’s brother. Asel Temirbekova, prima ballerina (Odette and Odile, Giselle). Nurzhamal Abdieva (Janyl).

The house

The theatre opened in 1959 in its building on Theatre Square, in the Soviet centre of Talas, the first in the city with a fly tower. Its first prima ballerina, Aikan Borubai kyzy, danced here from 1959 to 1975; the rehearsal studio is named after her.

Ballet company of 46, opera chorus of 38, orchestra of 62. Artistic director: Erkin Asanov. Chief conductor: Dmitry Ten.
