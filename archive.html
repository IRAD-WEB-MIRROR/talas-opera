Past seasons

The theatre opened on Theatre Square in 1959 and has played every season since. The record below comes from the theatre’s museum: the posters, the programmes and the prompt books.

  • Aikan’s years · 1959–1975
    Director: Akmatbek Usubaliev (1959–1971), Jumagul Sarieva (1971–1983). Chief conductor: Pavel Grinberg (1959–1977).
  • The full house · 1976–1991
    Director: Jumagul Sarieva (1971–1983), Ivan Medvedev (1983–1994). Chief conductor: Kubanych Toktosunov (1977–1995).
  • A smaller company · 1992–2006
    Director: Ivan Medvedev (until 1994), Gulnara Turdubaeva (1994–2009). Chief conductor: Kubanych Toktosunov (until 1995), Sergei Ten (1995–2009).
  • Kylych Karabekov · 2007–2017
    Director: Gulnara Turdubaeva (until 2009), Almaz Isakov (2009–2015). Chief conductor: Dmitry Ten (since 2009).
  • Janyl again · 2018–2026
    Artistic director: Erkin Asanov (since 2015). Chief conductor: Dmitry Ten.
