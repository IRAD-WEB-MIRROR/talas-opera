The full house, 1976–1991

Director: Jumagul Sarieva (1971–1983), Ivan Medvedev (1983–1994). Chief conductor: Kubanych Toktosunov (1977–1995).

The company was at its largest in these years, over 300 people. Aizada Kemel joined the opera in 1982 and was named People’s Artist in 1989.
