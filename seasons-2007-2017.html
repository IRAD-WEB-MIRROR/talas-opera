Kylych Karabekov, 2007–2017

Director: Gulnara Turdubaeva (until 2009), Almaz Isakov (2009–2015). Chief conductor: Dmitry Ten (since 2009).

After the dark spring of 2007 the theatre found a new generation of dancers in its own school. Kylych Karabekov danced as a guest at the Bolshoi in 2012.
