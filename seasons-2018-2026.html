Janyl again, 2018–2026

Artistic director: Erkin Asanov (since 2015). Chief conductor: Dmitry Ten.

In 2018 the theatre revived Janyl, with Nurzhamal Abdieva in the title role, and the Körköm Foundation began to pay for the Ballet School’s shoes, physiotherapist and summer places.
